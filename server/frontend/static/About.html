About Dealerships National Network

Founded in 2018, Dealerships is America's premier digital automotive marketplace, connecting discerning drivers with certified automotive dealerships across all 50 states. We leverage state-of-the-art microservices and AI-powered sentiment analysis to ensure that every buyer receives honest reviews, transparent pricing, and an exceptional vehicle ownership journey.
